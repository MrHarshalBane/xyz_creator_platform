XYZ Compliance Framework

How we operate a micro-transaction engine within Indian laws:

1. The Nodal/Escrow Structure
All user deposits and advertiser dues are stored in a **Partner Bank Nodal Account** monitored under RBI directions. XYZ acts as a Payment Intermediary, never holding proprietary control over user balances.
2. PAN Verification & TDS Logic
Real-time PAN Card checks are executed before bank withdrawals. Under Section 194R/194S of the IT Act, a 1% TDS is computed and reported directly to the Income Tax Department of India for creator earnings exceeding statutory limits.
3. Aggregate UPI Clearing Rails
Rather than hitting national payment switches for individual 0.1 paisa balances, XYZ groups transactions internally. Settlements are cleared via automated NPCI/UPI 2.0 bulk payout hooks, keeping transaction overhead extremely low (₹0.15 flat per successful batch settlement).
